The form asks for little, but the more of it you fill in, the fewer questions come back and the closer the first design lands.

## The fields, one by one

| Field | What to put there |
|---|---|
| What do you need? | A short name for the thing: *wall mount for my router*, *replacement knob for a gas hob*. |
| What is it for? | What it holds, attaches to or protects, and where it lives: indoors, outdoors, in a bathroom, near a heater, in a car. |
| Sizes it must fit | The measurements that matter, in millimetres. See *How to measure for a part*. |
| How many | How many copies you want. Say if you might want more later. |
| Material | PLA, PETG, TPU, ASA, or *Not sure* and one is recommended for you. |
| Color | Any, or a preference. Up to {printer.toolheads} colours can be combined in one part. |
| When do you need it? | A date, if there is one. Say *no rush* if there isn't. |
| Photos | Photos of the object and the place it goes, with a ruler in shot. |
| Additional information | Anything else: how much load it takes, what it must not cover, a link to something similar. |

## Say what it must do, not how to build it

The most useful requests describe the problem and the constraints, and leave the shape to the design:

::: quote A good request
"Three TV remotes keep disappearing down the side of the couch. I'd like something that hangs over the arm and holds them upright. The arm is 140 mm across the top and padded, and I don't want screws or sticky pads anywhere. Remotes are 45 × 20 × 180, 40 × 18 × 150 and 38 × 15 × 140 mm."
:::

That gives the sizes that matter, the surface it sits on, and the one hard constraint (nothing that marks the couch). It is enough to design from.

## Things worth saying if they are true

- **It has to take weight.** Say roughly how much, and in which direction.
- **It must come apart** for cleaning, or to change a battery.
- **It lives outside**, in a car, in a bathroom, or near an oven.
- **It has to match** something you already own: give the colour or send a photo.
- **It touches food or skin.** Say so; it changes the material and the finish.

::: warn One thing at a time
One request, one part. If you need three unrelated things, raise three requests: each gets its own design, quote and schedule, and none of them waits on the others.
:::
