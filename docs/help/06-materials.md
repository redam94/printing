## The four filaments

| Material | Good for | Heat | Outdoors | Feel | Filament cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PLA | Indoor parts, holders, organisers, decorative pieces, prototypes | Softens around 55 °C — not in a parked car | No | Stiff, crisp detail, widest colour choice | {price.pla_kg}/kg |
| PETG | Kitchens, bathrooms, parts that get wet or warm, clips that flex a little | About 75 °C | Some | Tougher, slightly glossy, less brittle | {price.petg_kg}/kg |
| TPU | Bumpers, feet, grips, gaskets, sleeves, anything squishy | About 80 °C | Yes | Rubbery, bends and springs back | {price.tpu_kg}/kg |
| ASA | Garden, car exteriors, anything in sunlight | About 95 °C | Yes | Matte, tough, UV-stable | {price.asa_kg}/kg |

Filament is rarely the biggest part of a quote, so choose for the job rather than the price.

## Which one for your part

| Your situation | Choose |
|---|---|
| A holder, stand or organiser for a desk or shelf | PLA |
| Something in a bathroom, kitchen or near a kettle | PETG |
| Left in a car, or on a sunny windowsill | ASA (PETG at a pinch) |
| Clips onto something and must spring | PETG |
| Needs to grip, cushion or seal | TPU |
| Screwed to a wall and holding real weight | PETG, with thicker walls |
| Fully outside, all year | ASA |
| A one-off to check the shape and size | PLA, every time |

## What printed plastic is not

- **Not dishwasher-safe.** Hand-wash printed parts in cool water; a dishwasher cycle will soften and warp most of them.
- **Not food-safe as printed.** Layer lines hold bacteria and cannot be sterilised, so printed parts are fine for dry, brief contact (a biscuit cutter, a funnel you rinse) and wrong for storing food or drinking from.
- **Not as strong across the layers.** A printed part is strongest along its layer lines. Parts are oriented on the printer to put the load in the strong direction, which is one of the things the design step is for.
- **Not a metal part.** Threads smaller than M6 are printed as a pocket for a brass insert or a captive nut instead of as plastic threads.

::: tip Mixing materials in one part
The printer carries {printer.toolheads} filaments at once, so a single part can combine a rigid body with a soft TPU foot or pad, or use a second colour for lettering. Ask for it in the request.
