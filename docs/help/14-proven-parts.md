A case for your board is not drawn from a blank screen. The pockets for the brass inserts, the board's hole pattern, the vent slots, the lid and the snap fit are all **components from a shared library**: parametric building blocks that have been printed, measured and, where a finished part used them, reported on. Only the shape around your object is new.

## What "proven" means

A component is marked as printed in a material when a part that used it came off the printer and was reported working. That report is written down against the design and against every component the design used, so the next design that reaches for the same component knows what to expect. A failed print does the opposite: the component is marked, the design goes back to the drawing board, and the same mistake is not made twice. Both kinds of report are public on the [track record page]({site}prints.html).

Not every component has been printed yet. When a design uses one that has not, the quote says so, and it is treated as the thing to check first when the part comes off the printer.

## What it does for you

- **Fewer surprises.** A pocket that has held a heat-set insert in a finished part holds it the same way in yours. Connector windows come from the board's drawing, not from a guess.
- **A smaller design fee.** Fitting proven parts around your measurements is quick, which is why most requests carry the *fitted to your object* fee rather than the *from scratch* one. See *Prices and turnaround*.
- **Faster revisions.** Because the parts are parametric, "2 mm wider" or "M4 instead of M3" is a change to a number, not a redraw. A revision usually comes back within a day.
- **A written record.** Every design page on [the site]({site}) lists the components it used and the materials they have been printed in, and your quote's measurement sheet lists the same for your part.

::: tip Ask for something that already exists
If a design on the work page is close to what you need, say so in the request. Printed as it is, there is no design fee at all; sized to your object, it is the small fee, and you still see the revised model before anything is printed.
:::
