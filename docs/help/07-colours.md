## Colours

The portal form offers: any / no preference, black, white, grey, red, blue, green, yellow, orange, and multi-colour (up to {printer.toolheads} colours in one part).

- **Black and white** are always in stock and suit most functional parts.
- **A specific shade**, or a match to something you own: say so and send a photo. It may take a few days longer to obtain.
- **Multi-colour** puts up to {printer.toolheads} filaments in one part, for lettering, markings or a two-tone body. It adds a little printing time, because the printer swaps filament at every colour change.

## What the finish looks like

Parts are built from {printer.layer} layers with a {printer.nozzle} nozzle, so:

- **Flat top and bottom faces** look smooth and slightly satin.
- **Vertical walls** show fine horizontal lines — visible up close, not rough to the touch.
- **Curves that slope gently** show the layer steps most; a sharp edge or a steep wall shows them least.
- **The underside**, where it met the print bed, is the smoothest face of all and takes on the bed's texture.

Every part is cleaned up before it is handed over: supports removed, stray strands trimmed, the bed edge deburred.

## If you want it smoother

- **Ask for the part to be oriented for looks.** The face you care about can usually be put on the bed or made a vertical wall. Say which face shows.
- **Sanding and priming** is possible on PLA and PETG for a paint-ready surface. It is hand work, so it is quoted separately.
- **Paint it yourself:** scuff with fine sandpaper, use a plastic primer, then any acrylic or model paint.
- **Glue:** cyanoacrylate (superglue) holds PLA and PETG well. TPU needs a flexible adhesive.

::: tip Prototype first, pretty second
When a part has to both fit and look right, the usual path is one plain PLA print to check the fit, then the final in the colour and material you want. Say so in the request and both are planned into the quote.
:::
