Every part is designed and checked with you before anything is printed. You never pay for a surprise.

## The five steps

1. **You raise a request** on the [portal]({portal}). Say what you need, what it is for, and the sizes it has to fit. A photo with a ruler in it helps more than any description.
2. **It gets designed.** The part is modelled from scratch around your measurements. If something important is missing, you get a question on the request before any drawing starts.
3. **You get a quote.** A comment arrives on the request, and by e-mail, with a 3D viewer file, renders, a measurement sheet, a price and a ready-by date.
4. **You confirm or ask for changes.** Reply `CONFIRM` to go ahead, or say what should be different. A revised design comes back the same way, as many times as it takes.
5. **It is printed**, checked, and handed over. The request is resolved once the part is in your hands.

## What the status on your request means

| Status | What is happening | Who has the ball |
|---|---|---|
| To Do | The request has arrived and is queued | Us |
| In Progress | Being designed, or being printed after you confirmed | Us |
| Pending | A quote is waiting for your reply | You |
| Done | Finished, or cancelled at your request | Nobody |

Asking for a change moves the request back to **In Progress** and you get a new quote. Cancelling closes it as **Done**; nothing is charged for a request you cancel before confirming.

## How long each step takes

- **First quote:** usually two to three working days after the request, sooner for something simple.
- **A revision:** usually a day, because the design already exists.
- **Printing:** {price.queue_days} days in the queue plus the print itself. A palm-sized part prints in two to four hours; a large one can run overnight. Your quote gives the real estimate.

::: tip Everything stays on the request
The portal keeps the whole history: your original description, every quote, every reply. If you lose the e-mail, open the request on the portal and it is all there.
:::
