Hi Matthew,

Here is the design for your request "[TEST] Remote control holder for the side of the couch" (PRINT-1), revision 1.
The attached 3D viewer opens in any browser: orbit it, section it, and check every measurement against the spot it goes in.

This is one piece that sits over the couch arm: a flat saddle across the top, a short leg down the seat side, and a block of three pockets hanging down the outside. No screws or glue; the remotes' weight holds it against the arm. Every edge that touches the fabric is rounded, and the underside has four shallow seats for stick-on felt dots (12 mm) so nothing rubs the upholstery. Each pocket is 2 mm bigger than the remote and half its length deep, so the top half of each remote stays in reach. Two things I assumed and would like you to check: (1) I read 'couch arm 150 wide, 25 thick' as a 150 mm wide, roughly flat arm top, with the 25 mm being the padding; if the arm is rounded or narrower, tell me its width at the top and I will adjust the saddle. (2) The caddy runs 140 mm along the arm; say if you want it shorter.
