## Brief
**Subject:** Ribbed cylindrical vase with bulbous shoulder

**Style:** ribbed, cylindrical, minimalist, geometric, modern, clean

**Form:** Tall cylindrical body with a dramatically full, bulging shoulder that extends well beyond the cylinder diameter. The shoulder tapers smoothly into a wide elliptical opening at top. The overall proportions are roughly 2:1 height-to-width. Vertically symmetrical around the central axis; the body maintains consistent diameter below the shoulder then widens into it.

**Surface:** Evenly-spaced vertical ribs or fluting with uniform depth running the full height from shoulder to base; ribs are crisp with sharp edges, no heavy fillets. The negative spaces between ribs are as prominent as the ribs themselves. Smooth, gently curved rim at the mouth opening.

**Features to borrow:**
- Pronounced bulging shoulder
- Evenly-spaced vertical fluting/ribs
- Wide elliptical mouth opening
- Sharp-edged rib geometry
- Tall proportioned cylinder

**Materials / colours:** Ceramic or clay in warm taupe-brown with cream-colored negative spaces creating strong visual rhythm in the ribbed pattern.

**Print notes:** Shoulder overhang will require support structures. Ribs are relatively thin and may need orientation with vertical axis up and base on bed. Consider supports under the shoulder bulge and possibly internal bracing for thin rib walls depending on print angle.

**build123d hints:**
- Revolve a 2D profile that includes the shoulder bulge curve from a center line
- Linear pattern of rectangular cuts or ribs down the body (extrude and boolean subtract)
- Alternatively, revolve with symmetric shoulder, then use circular or linear polar pattern of rib geometry
- Keep fillet radius small or zero on ribs to maintain sharp edges
- Shell the solid if printing hollow to manage wall thickness
