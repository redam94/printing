Summary

This bistable compliant mechanism was developed by the BYU Compliant Mechanisms Research group (compliantmechanisms.byu.edu). This model shows the bistable behavior that can be achieved by using a single print compliant mechanism.

## Reading
**Subject:** Bistable compliant beam pair with central plunger and living hinges

**Mechanism:** bistable snap-through parallel flexure with living hinges

**Principle:** Two parallel angled beams, each connected to a central T-shaped plunger via living hinges, form two stable equilibrium positions with beams bent upward or downward. The beams store elastic energy and provide restoring force; pressing the plunger triggers snap-through motion between states. The living hinges (0.5 mm) enable the flexure while the angled geometry (8 deg) and beam width (5 mm) govern the snap-through threshold and bistability.

**Key dimensions:**
- living hinge width 0.5 mm (measured)
- beam width in plane 5.0 mm (measured)
- Z depth / print height 6.35 mm (measured)
- beam angle ~8 degrees (estimated from renders)
- beam span per side ~35–40 mm (estimated from renders)
- Original bbox 119.4 × 89.21 × 6.35 mm (measured)
- Thinner_walls bbox 104.15 × 72.21 × 6.35 mm (measured)
- Original thickest member 21.98 mm (measured)
- Thinner_walls thickest member 12.75 mm (measured)
- Thinner_walls variant: frame/wall thickness reduced ~13%, thickest member 41% thinner than Original, same hinge and beam widths

**Features to borrow:**
- two parallel angled beams converging toward center plunger
- 0.5 mm living hinges connecting beams to central plunger
- 0.5 mm living hinges at beam anchors to frame
- central T-shaped plunger with circular end stop for actuating
- symmetric top and bottom beam pairs
- flat rectangular frame with fixed left and right anchors
- single monolithic print, minimal supports needed
- snap-through bistable action between two stable positions

**Print notes:** Flat bed orientation with 6.35 mm Z-height (single layer height is critical for snap-through). Both variants print as single monolithic pieces with minimal support material. Material not specified on Printables page but FDM-compatible; printed example shown in tan/brown color. No failures reported by designer. Tolerances critical at 0.5 mm living hinges and 5.0 mm beams; snap-through behavior depends on hinge width being very precise (roughly at nozzle width or narrower for reliable bistability).

**build123d hints:**
- sketch two parallel angled lines from fixed anchors converging toward center, constrained at 8 deg angle and span 35–40 mm per side
- extrude beam cross-section (5.0 mm width × 6.35 mm depth) along each angled line using swept profile or loft
- create living hinges (0.5 mm width × 6.35 mm depth) at four locations: where each beam meets the central plunger (2×) and where each beam anchors to frame (2×)
- extrude central T-shaped or cross-shaped plunger (1–2 mm width) perpendicular to flexure plane, spanning between beam hinges
- add circular end stop (5–8 mm diameter) at plunger ends visible in top-view photo
- create rectangular frame around entire assembly with wall thickness 3–5 mm
- mirror the top beam pair to the bottom to create symmetric bistable pair
- optional fillet at beam-to-hinge transitions (0.1–0.2 mm radius) to reduce stress concentration for FDM durability

**Library map:**
- mechanisms.parallel_flexure: the angled beam pair forming the flexure structure
- mechanisms.living_hinge: the 0.5 mm thin necks connecting beams to plunger and anchors
- mechanisms.snap_fit: the snap-through bistable behavior and energy storage
- gap: mechanisms.bistable_beam_pair: the specific angled-pair geometry optimized for minimal printable bistable switch
