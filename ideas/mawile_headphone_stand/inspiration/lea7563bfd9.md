## Brief
**Subject:** Mawile Pokémon character, emphasizing oversized black jaw-mane for headphone stand inspiration

**Style:** stylized, geometric, sharp-toothed, sculptural, character-based

**Form:** Compact yellow body supporting a dramatically oversized black jaw structure that sweeps upward and curves backward—the jaw is roughly 2.5–3× the body's height. The jaw forms a smooth, tapering arc that rises from the rear of the head and curves forward slightly, like an exaggerated crocodile snout but oriented vertically. Vertically symmetrical along the central axis.

**Surface:** Glossy, hard-edged surfaces with no organic softness. The jaw is polished black; the body cream-yellow. White triangular teeth are sharp and evenly spaced—approximately 4–5 visible teeth along the jaw's leading edge, each a small isosceles triangle with crisp points. Teeth create visual rhythm along the jaw's curve.

**Features to borrow:**
- oversized black jaw-mane sweeping upward from the body
- sharp white triangular teeth arranged along jaw edge
- smooth tapering curve of the jaw from back to front
- compact body-to-jaw size contrast

**Materials / colours:** Yellow/pale cream body, pure black jaw, white enamel-like tooth details; appears to be rendered plastic or enamel.

**Print notes:** Significant overhang: the swept jaw curves above the body and will require support structures, especially near the apex. The connection between body and jaw is thin and could be fragile in FDM; consider wall thickness ≥2mm there. Orient with the body on the bed, jaw pointing upward.

**build123d hints:**
- sweep or loft for the curved jaw profile along a path
- linear pattern of scaled cubes or cylinders (rotated) for sharp teeth
- fillet or chamfer edges lightly for print sharpness retention
- offset shell inward by 1–2mm for wall strength if printing hollow
